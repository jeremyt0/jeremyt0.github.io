# Welcome to My Blog

Hello! I'm excited to start this blog as a place to share my thoughts on software engineering, technology, and life experiences.

## What to Expect

This blog will cover:
- **Software Engineering**: Best practices, lessons learned, and technical deep dives
- **Technology**: Reviews of tools, frameworks, and emerging trends
- **Personal Growth**: Reflections on learning, career development, and work-life balance

## Why Now?

After working in startups and now at a travel tech company, I've accumulated many insights that I want to share. Writing helps me organize my thoughts and hopefully helps others on similar journeys.

## Technical Details

This site is built with **Jekyll** and hosted on **GitHub Pages**. I migrated from Astro to Jekyll to have a simpler, blog-focused setup with native GitHub Pages support.

Stay tuned for more posts! If you have any questions or topics you'd like me to cover, feel free to reach out.
